Task Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Task Tracker is a private, offline-first personal planning app. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information or app activity. The app has no user accounts, analytics, or advertising, and the developer does not operate a server that receives your data.

Data stored on your device

Work cycles, sprints, tasks, workflow settings, and other content you enter are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot access this information. Your device operating system may include app data in a device backup according to your own iCloud, Google, or device backup settings.

Backups and sharing

If you choose Export Data, Task Tracker creates a JSON backup and opens your device's share sheet. The backup is sent only to the destination you select. Importing a backup reads the file you choose and stores its contents locally. Task Tracker does not receive a copy of exported or imported data.

Optional sync to your own server

Sync is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings, Task Tracker sends your work cycles, sprints, tasks, and workflow settings over HTTPS only to the server address you enter, using the sync key you provide. The developer does not provide this server and does not receive a copy of your data. Anyone who has your server address and sync key can read and change the synced data, so keep the key private.

You can turn sync off at any time. "Delete cloud copy" in Settings deletes the data on your server and turns sync off; data on your device stays. If you connect your server to other tools, such as AI assistants, those tools' own privacy policies apply to the data they access.

Data deletion and changes

You can delete individual tasks or work cycles inside the app. Uninstalling Task Tracker removes its locally stored data, subject to any backups managed by your device or any backup files you exported. If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will be shown here.

For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on Task Tracker's App Store or Google Play listing.
